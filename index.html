About this list
Compiled from the batch lists and reunion records available to the organiser as of 28 October 2026. Names retain the spelling supplied; branch labels have been standardised. This is a working community directory, not an official college roll.

*Branch strengths are organiser-supplied estimates: Electrical and Mechanical 60 each; Ceramic and Mining 15 each; all other branches 30 each. These are coverage guides, not independently verified roll counts.

Only names and branches are published. Contact information and personal reunion details are not included. Missing name or correction? Please tell the reunion organiser.
